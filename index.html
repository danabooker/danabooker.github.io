About Me

Welcome! My name is Dana Booker, and I am a faculty member in the Educational Psychology department at the University of North Texas. I am passionate about teaching and student knowledge acquisition. I believe myself to be a catalyst for student success from the classroom to their careers. I am an enthusiastic advocate for children, youth, and families.
