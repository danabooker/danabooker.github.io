About Me

I hold a Ph.D. in Educational Psychology with a concentration in Human Development and Family Science from the University of North Texas. My professional expertise lies in child advocacy, multi-system collaboration focused on parent and early childhood prevention and intervention strategies, and professional development for the early childhood and parent education workforce. Previously, I worked with Prevent Child Abuse Texas, an experience that deepened my passion for prevention and intervention work. I am also a trained program evaluator through the American Evaluation Association, with experience evaluating programs across various sectors, including programmatic, community, and university settings. Originally from Albuquerque, New Mexico, I earned both my B.A. in Psychology and M.S. in Educational Psychology from the University of New Mexico.
