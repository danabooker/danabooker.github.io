My Teachings

I am a forever student and value learning environments that are student-centered. Within my teaching approach, I look for opportunities to establish collaborations to fortify students’ learning experiences. For me, collaboration in learning has been one of the most enriching aspects in my sixteen years of teaching and in my many years as a student.

Student-Centered Teaching Strategies

I aim to establish learning collaborations within my courses by:

  1. Building clear expectations for learning;
  2. Maximizing opportunities to incorporate students’ prior knowledge and experiences;
  3. Allowing for critical reflection.

Academic Teaching Background

  1. I have taught at the post-secondary education for over a decade.
  2. I have taught at both undergraduate and graduate level courses in this time span.
  3. I have taught both in-person and online courses.
